# Privacy Bundle

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Not yet fully live.** The bundle is not on sale yet.
{% endhint %}

One monthly add-on pairing Kokio eSIM data with Mysterium VPN. Allowances will reset each billing month.

| Plan | eSIM data | VPN | Devices |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Travel Shield | 3 GB regional | 25 GB | 3 |
| Global Shield | 10 GB global | 100 GB | 5 |
| Nomad Shield | 25 GB global | 250 GB | 10 |

It is not unlimited data or anonymous banking, and does not change your Zold account or identity. Check eSIM region availability in the app when activating.
