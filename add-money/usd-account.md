# Your US dollar account

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Not yet fully live.** Request an account at **Accounts → Open an account → US dollar**. It will be recorded and opened when the rail is ready; the app will tell you.
{% endhint %}

You get an account number and routing number in your name. **Bridge**, a licensed money transmitter, issues them, converts incoming dollars to USDC and sends payouts to US banks. Your euro account is separate.

| Action | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Receive | ACH and domestic wire; free. |
| Hold | USDC in the same Zold account as your euros; issued by Circle and backed by reserves. |
| Pay out | ACH or wire to a US bank; fixed fee shown on the quote. |
| Convert | Euro ↔ dollar at the quote's rate and margin; approve with your passkey. Settles in seconds and appears in **Activity**. |

- **Verification**: Bridge runs its own identity check; Monerium's does not replace it. **Settings** lists each partner's status.
- **Eligibility**: follows Bridge's rules for your country. US persons cannot hold a Zold account; this account is for people and businesses outside the US who receive or pay dollars.
