# Gnosis Pay card

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Not yet fully live.** You can connect an existing Gnosis Pay account and see its cards, balance and transactions. Applying for a card and topping it up from Zold are coming.
{% endhint %}

Gnosis Pay issues the Visa card, runs its own identity check and holds its separate card account. A card payment does not ask for Face ID or your fingerprint; Zold's approval rule applies to transfers from your Zold account. The card can spend what you load onto its account.

## Connect

Profile → **Card → Sign in with your wallet**. Confirm the sign-in message in your browser wallet, using the wallet that owns your Gnosis Pay account. Zold never sees its password or key.

Zold shows your cards and status, card-account balance labelled with its read time, and recent transactions. They are snapshots; press **Refresh** for an update. The session stays only in browser memory; reload means signing in again.

Gnosis Pay runs its own verification and issues the card. Zold displays it as a Gnosis Pay card, not a Zold card.
