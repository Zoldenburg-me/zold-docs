# Create your account

Open **zoldhq.com/app** and choose **Open your account**.

## Steps

1. Enter your country of residence and every citizenship you hold.
2. Answer the US-person question. A US citizen, Green Card holder or US tax resident cannot open an account, wherever they live. Companies also say whether they are US-incorporated or have a US owner of 25% or more. A “yes” closes the account path and is recorded.
3. Check your eligibility. Most EEA, UK and Swiss residents can open a full account. Elsewhere, you may get an IBAN if Monerium serves your country; most countries it does not serve get a wallet account with no IBAN or identity check. Zold is unavailable to residents or citizens of sanctioned countries and some other countries. The app tells you before account creation. See [Supported countries](../reference/supported-countries.md).
4. Set up a passkey with Face ID, fingerprint, device PIN, Windows Hello, your phone or a security key. On a computer, use its password, scan the browser's QR code with your phone or use a security key. There is no password; your key stays in secure hardware or the key manager you choose.
5. If a passkey prompt stays open, use **Cancel** or close the existing prompt, then reload. Prompts stay open for two minutes.
6. Your account is created for free, within seconds of setting up the key. Its address is fixed.
7. Connect Monerium to get an IBAN, or continue with a wallet account. Bank transfers require an IBAN.

The passkey is the only approver on your account; Zold cannot replace it. Set up [recovery](security-and-recovery.md) and check whether your passkey syncs through iCloud Keychain, Google Password Manager or another password manager. If it does not, losing the device means recovery.

## Your profile

**Profile** holds your name, Zold tag, payment link, account details, security and settings. You can change your name until Monerium verifies you; then it is locked as the IBAN holder's name. Company accounts use the company's name.
