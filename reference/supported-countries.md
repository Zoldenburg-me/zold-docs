# Supported countries

Who may open an account at all is on [Eligibility](eligibility.md).

## Where you can hold an account

| You live in | You get |
| --- | --- |
| EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland | Full account: IBAN, euros and payments. Card eligibility depends on the issuer. |
| Other countries with IBAN service | IBAN and euro account; no card. |
| India | Wallet account. Collections through an Indian partner are not yet fully live. |
| Other countries we serve, for example Nigeria, Kenya or South Africa | Wallet account. |

Zold checks at sign-up which one you get.

**Wallet account**: no IBAN, no bank transfers in or out, no identity check. You can receive and hold USDC and euros, convert dollars to euros, use payment pages and links, send invoices, keep books and set up recovery. If your identity check for an IBAN fails, the wallet account stays open.

## Where you can send money

| Destination | Method | Currency | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SEPA countries | Bank transfer to IBAN | EUR | Open |
| Kenya | MoneyGram cash pickup | KES | Not yet fully live |
| Kenya | M-Pesa | KES | Not yet fully live |
| United States | ACH / wire from USD account | USD | Opening soon |
| United Kingdom | Faster Payments | GBP | Not yet fully live |
| Switzerland | Swiss IBAN | CHF | Not yet fully live |
| Nigeria | NIP bank transfer (NUBAN) | NGN | Not yet fully live |

The send flow offers only open destinations.
