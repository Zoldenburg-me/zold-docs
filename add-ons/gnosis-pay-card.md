# Gnosis Pay card

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Not yet fully live.** You can connect an existing Gnosis Pay account and see its cards, balance and transactions. Applying for a card and topping it up from Zold are coming.
{% endhint %}

Gnosis Pay issues the Visa card, runs its own identity check and holds its separate card account; Zold shows it as a Gnosis Pay card, not a Zold card. A card payment asks for no Face ID or fingerprint; Zold's approval rule applies to transfers from your Zold account. The card can spend what you load onto its account.

## Connect

Profile → **Card → Sign in with your wallet**. Confirm the sign-in message in the browser wallet that owns your Gnosis Pay account. Zold never sees its password or key.

You see cards and status, card-account balance and recent transactions as snapshots; press **Refresh** to update. The session stays only in browser memory; reload means signing in again.
