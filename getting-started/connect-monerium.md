# Get your IBAN

Your IBAN comes with one identity check and one passkey approval. Zold runs no separate identity check and does not pass on your signup details.

## Steps

1. After setting up your passkey, choose **Continue with Monerium**, our IBAN partner.
2. Sign in or open an account there and complete its check: details, document and liveness. Usually minutes; it emails you if it needs more.
3. Approve the connection and return to Zold.
4. Press **Get your IBAN** and approve with your passkey.

You can leave during the check; the IBAN appears on Home when ready.

## Skipped it at signup?

Open **Profile → Settings → Monerium**. In Zold Business (company owner only), it is under **Accounts** or **Settings → Monerium**.

Press **Continue with Monerium**, sign in there, then follow steps 3–4 above.

A company account then lands on **Accounts**: press **Connect the company's IBAN**.

## Already have a Monerium IBAN?

Each Monerium profile has one IBAN. When you connect, Zold offers to move it here: type `MOVE`, press **Move IBAN to Zold** and approve with your passkey. Payers keep the same IBAN; your old wallet stops receiving. You can move it back through Monerium.

## Use your own Monerium API keys

On the Monerium screen, under **Developer option: your own API keys**, enter your developer app's client ID and secret. Zold checks the pair and stores it encrypted; the secret is never shown again.

## Good to know

- **Before the IBAN**: your account works as a wallet: hold dollars and euros, convert dollars to euros, get paid through your payment page, send invoices. Bank transfers need the IBAN.
- **Declined**: your wallet stays open without an IBAN. Connecting Monerium still works, but no IBAN is offered. Questions: support@zoldhq.com.
- **Under review**: Monerium is still checking your account. You can connect it under **Settings → Monerium**, which says the account is under review and no IBAN is issued yet. With **Continue with Monerium**, Zold then takes you to Home; with your own API keys, it shows "Monerium is still reviewing this account" with **Back to Home**. On Home the IBAN step reads "Monerium is still reviewing this account. No IBAN yet." with nothing to press; before you connect, the identity step reads "Under review at Monerium." Your account details, **Get paid**, **Send** and **Coming soon** say the same, with no **Verify your identity** and no **Get your IBAN**. This stays until Zold confirms Monerium's approval on your account; it does not change by itself when Monerium finishes its review. Questions: **Email support** on the review screen, or support@zoldhq.com.
- **Activation failed**: press **Get your IBAN** again.
- **Removing API keys**: your IBAN stays on record but deposits and payouts pause.
- **Changing the connection**: needs your passkey and signs you out on other devices.
