# Create a personal account

For an account in your own name. For a company, see [Create a business account](create-a-business-account.md).

## Steps

1. Open **zoldhq.com/app**, choose **Create an account**, then **Personal**.
2. Enter:
   - **Name**: as on your ID.
   - **Email**: used for recovery and sign-in help.
   - **Residence country**.
   - **US question**: are you a US citizen, Green Card holder or US tax resident?
   - Accept Zold's Terms.
3. Add a way back in: **Continue with Google**, **Continue with Apple** or **Use my email**, or **Skip for now**.
4. Set up your passkey (Face ID, fingerprint, device PIN, Windows Hello, your phone or a security key), then **approve once more**.
5. On Home, finish **Confirm your email** and [Get your IBAN](connect-monerium.md).

{% hint style="info" %}
A "yes" to the US question closes the account path wherever you live, and the answer is recorded.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Not yet fully live.** Step 3 (Google, Apple or email recovery) is not offered yet. See [Security and recovery](security-and-recovery.md#your-own-backup-login).
{% endhint %}

## Good to know

- **Who can join**: Zold is unavailable to US persons, residents of sanctioned countries, and residents of some countries. See [Eligibility](../reference/eligibility.md).
- **Passkey**: the only approver; Zold cannot replace it. Without sync (iCloud Keychain, Google Password Manager or another), losing the device means recovery.
- **Recovery helper**: can move your account to a new phone, never send money.
- **Cost**: account creation is free and fixes your address.
- **Before your IBAN**: Zold works as a wallet: hold dollars and euros, get paid through your payment page, send invoices. Bank transfers need the IBAN. Wallet accounts have no IBAN step.
- **Name**: editable in **Profile** until your identity is verified; then locked as the IBAN holder's name.
