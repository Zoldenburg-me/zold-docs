# Tracking and receipts

## Activity and details

**Activity** lists deposits and transfers, newest first, with a status on each row. 

- **Bank deposit**: sender, reference and date.
- **Crypto deposit**: sending wallet, network date and current asset. Converted dollars show euros received; held dollars offer **Convert to euros**.
- **Transfer**: amount sent and received, rate, fee, recipient, reference or pickup code, and a timeline of states reached. Cash pickups also show the MoneyGram reference and **Refresh status**.

A conversion is your own money changing form, not another received payment. Technical details link to Basescan.

## Share a receipt

Open a transfer after it starts moving and choose **Share receipt**. Pick what the link reveals:

| Field | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| Your or recipient name | Full, first, last or none |
| Payout account | Full, short or hidden |
| Amounts | Both, sent or received |

Optional switches add **Rate, fee & margin**, **Your reference** and **Settlement route**. In Business, open **Statements → Receipts for payments → Share**.

Press **Create link**. The link appears under **Send this link**, with **Copy link**, and **Share link** where your browser offers it. If the share sheet does not open, use **Copy link**. The address of the Share receipt screen itself opens only for you.

- **Access**: anyone with the link can open it without an account. Withheld fields are not sent.
- **A sent link never changes**: what a link shows stays as it was made. Change a setting while a link is live and the button becomes **Make new link**; the live link is hidden until you press it or change the settings back. **Make new link** makes a new link with the new settings and turns the old one off: anyone with the old link sees that it was turned off.
- **One live link per transfer**: making a new link always turns the previous one off. If Zold's answer doesn't arrive, the screen reads which link is live and shows it. If it can't read that either, the settings stay locked and no link is shown; open the receipt again. **Turn this link off** waits while a new link is being made.
- **Expiry**: links expire after **30 days** and can be revoked; visitors see that it expired or was revoked. After a link expires, **Create link** makes a new one with the same settings; the old link stays off.
- **Care**: share links only with the intended person.
