# Your euro account

| You see | It means |
| --- | --- |
| **IBAN** | A euro IBAN issued by Monerium. Anyone can pay it by SEPA transfer. |
| **Balance** | Euros you hold and can spend: one euro in Zold is one euro. In-flight payments have already left it. |
| **Account address** | Where the euros sit and where USDC sent to you arrives. |

A bank transfer to your IBAN appears as euros. A SEPA payment redeems euros through Monerium and sends them to the payee's bank. **Activity** lists deposits and transfers; see [Tracking and receipts](../send-money/tracking-and-receipts.md).

## If your IBAN moves

Your Monerium login can point the IBAN to another account. Payments then stop reaching Zold and Home stops showing the IBAN as yours.

- Choose **Move IBAN back to Zold** and approve with your passkey.
- If Zold cannot see where it points, choose **Switch on your IBAN**; if Monerium says it pays elsewhere, Zold offers to move it back.
- You do not repeat identity verification.

## Who issues your euros

Monerium EMI ehf. is licensed by Iceland's Central Bank and passported across the EEA. Its e-money is regulated under MiCA, backed one-for-one by euros, and redeemable at par. Zold does not lend your euros; there is no interest, and e-money has no deposit guarantee.

Only you approve transfers, for each amount and recipient. Zold holds no key or standing permission to move your money. Zold pays network fees; you do not need another token.
