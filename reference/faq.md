# FAQ

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| **Is Zold a bank?** | No. Zold is software by Zoldenburg and has no banking licence. Monerium EMI ehf., licensed by Iceland's Central Bank and passported across the EEA, issues your IBAN and euros. |
| **Can anyone else send money from my account?** | No. Your passkey approves each amount and recipient. Email, a password or a caller posing as a bank employee cannot approve. Never approve a transfer you did not start. |
| **Can a thief use my phone?** | A transfer needs Face ID, fingerprint or the device passcode. Keep that passcode private. Use [recovery](../getting-started/security-and-recovery.md) if the phone is lost. |
| **What if Zold's servers are hacked?** | Servers hold no account key, but a compromised app could show a different payment to approve: check each one. A breach could expose stored data such as email and history. |
| **What does Zold cost?** | Every feature is free. No fee for sending or receiving euros or converting to and from USDC. Zold does not lend your money. |
| **Do I have to leave my bank?** | No. You can receive salary or client payments in Zold and send money to another bank. |
| **Can my employer refuse my German IBAN?** | In the euro area, an employer cannot refuse an IBAN because of its country. Your Zold IBAN works for salary like any other. |
| **Can people abroad pay me?** | Yes: euro bank transfer from Europe or USDC from outside Europe through a [payment link](../get-paid/payment-links.md). |
| **Do I earn interest or have deposit insurance?** | No interest. E-money is not covered by a deposit guarantee; Monerium safeguards the full issued value and you can redeem at par. |
| **Can Zold freeze or move my money?** | Zold holds no key and cannot approve transfers. Chosen Zoldenburg recovery can move your account after identity checks, never send money, and can be cancelled during the wait. With your own backup key, Zold has no part in it. Monerium may freeze e-money when law requires it. |
| **What if Zold shuts down?** | With your own backup key, use Safe tools at [app.safe.global](https://app.safe.global). Without one, your passkey works only through zoldhq.com. Your right to redeem euros is against Monerium, not Zold. |
| **Do I need crypto?** | No. Use euros; Zold pays network fees. |
| **Do card payments need Face ID?** | No. Gnosis Pay spends from its own card account; Zold's approval rule applies to Zold transfers. |
| **Can I use multiple devices?** | Yes, if your passkey syncs through iCloud Keychain, Google Password Manager or another password manager. Each device creates its own payment key. |
| **I lost my phone. What now?** | Follow [Security and recovery](../getting-started/security-and-recovery.md). If you chose Zoldenburg, start recovery at sign-in and contact support with its reference; it requires your confirmed bank account and identity check. Without recovery, only euros can be reclaimed from Monerium. |
| **Why was my quote refused?** | No fresh rate was available or the venue price was too far from market. Try again; Zold never uses an old rate. |
| **Why does my balance say “Balance unavailable”?** | The network read failed. Transfers are blocked until Zold can check your balance. Your money has not moved; reload later. |
| **Why is my transfer in Manual review?** | A person must review an issue, such as a partner failing after receiving funds. Funds are accounted for; you will be contacted. |
| **Can I hold dollars?** | A USD account with its own number and routing number is being added through Bridge. See [Your US dollar account](../add-money/usd-account.md). |
| **Can I pay in other currencies?** | SEPA transfers out are euros. Kenya cash pickup is not yet fully live; other rails depend on their accounts opening. |
| **Is my payment page private?** | No. It is public; see [Your payment page](../get-paid/payment-page.md). |
| **Does Zold give tax advice?** | No. Zold blocks invoices that fail the rules it encodes and states what it does not check. Your accountant decides the rest. |
| **Who do I contact?** | support@zoldhq.com. |
