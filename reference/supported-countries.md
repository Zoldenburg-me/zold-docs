# Supported countries

Eligibility depends on residence, every citizenship and US-person status. Companies also report incorporation country and whether an owner of **25% or more** is a US person.

## Holding an account

| You | Account |
| --- | --- |
| Resident in EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland | Full account: IBAN, euros and payments; card eligibility depends on the issuer. |
| Resident elsewhere where Monerium serves | IBAN and euro account; no card. |
| Resident in India | Wallet account; collections through an Indian partner are not yet fully live. |
| Resident where Monerium does not serve (for example Nigeria, Kenya or South Africa) | Wallet account. |
| Resident in Myanmar, Afghanistan, Libya, Yemen, Venezuela, Iraq or Lebanon | Not available; the app says Zold cannot offer an account there. |
| US citizen, Green Card holder or US tax resident, wherever they live; resident of a US territory | Not available. |
| Resident or citizen of Iran, North Korea, Syria, Cuba, Russia or Belarus | Not available. |

A wallet account has no IBAN or bank transfers in or out, and no identity check. It can receive and hold USDC and euros, convert dollars to euros, use payment pages and links, send invoices, keep books and set up recovery. If Monerium cannot verify you, the app says its banking partner cannot onboard you; the wallet account remains available. Monerium's served-country list can change; Zold checks it at sign-up.

## Sending money

| Destination | Method | Currency | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SEPA countries | Bank transfer to IBAN | EUR | Open |
| Kenya | MoneyGram cash pickup | KES | Not yet fully live |
| Kenya | M-Pesa | KES | Not yet fully live |
| United States | ACH / wire from USD account | USD | Opening soon through Bridge |
| United Kingdom | Faster Payments | GBP | Not yet fully live |
| Switzerland | Swiss IBAN | CHF | Not yet fully live |
| Nigeria | NIP bank transfer (NUBAN) | NGN | Not yet fully live |

The send flow offers only open destinations. A listed corridor is not a promise that it has moved money.
