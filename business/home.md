# Business Home

Home is the first screen of Zold Business: balance, what needs you, and the latest money in and out.

## How it works

- **Quick send**: pick a contact with a saved IBAN, enter an amount, press **Review transfer**. The New payment form opens filled in; nothing is sent until you submit it there. If Quick send is greyed out, the line under it says why.
- **Security**: whether your passkey signs for the account, whether recovery is on, and whether a backup key is added. **Add** and **Set up** open the app. Only the company's own login sees this card.
- **Set up**: the five steps to a working account, with "N of 5 done". It disappears when all five are done.
- **No IBAN yet**: the **Accounts** card says why. The company’s login presses **Connect Monerium**, then **Activate IBAN**; Monerium verifies the company before it issues an IBAN.
- **Recent activity**: **Pending** lists payments saved, in review, approved, or being sent. **Export CSV** downloads all transactions as a CSV file.

## Good to know

- **Couldn’t check**: when Zold cannot read who signs for the account or its recovery, the card says so. It never shows that as on or off.
- **Balance**: only the account backed by your own login shows a figure. Zold shows no combined company balance.
