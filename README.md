# Welcome to Zold

Zold is a euro account where only you approve transfers, with finance tools for teams.

- **Only you approve transfers:** your passkey approves the exact amount and recipient; Zold holds no key.
- **Two people for business payments:** with review on, one person drafts and another approves; accountants manage books, not money.
- **No rate, no asterisk:** Zold does not lend your euros. E-money is backed one-for-one, held in your name and has no deposit guarantee.
