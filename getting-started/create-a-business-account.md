# Create a business account

For a business: a company or a sole trader. For private money, see [Create a personal account](create-your-account.md).

## Steps

1. Open **zoldhq.com/app**, choose **Create an account**, then **Business**.
2. Answer three questions: your **name** (as on your ID), your **email**, and the business's **registered name**.
3. Add a way back in, or **Skip for now**.
4. Set up your passkey, then **approve once more**. Setting up sign-in accepts Zold's Terms.
5. Press **Answer** under **First: where you live**. Pick your country, then answer the two US questions (is the company incorporated in the US or 25% or more US-owned, and are you a US person?), then **Save**.
6. Create the organisation: check the **Name**, choose **Company** (GmbH, UG, AG, Ltd) or **Sole trader**, and enter its **Country**. Press **Create**.
7. On Home, finish the set-up list and [Get your IBAN](connect-monerium.md).

## Good to know

- **Who can join**: see [Eligibility](../reference/eligibility.md). A US incorporation or a US owner of 25% or more closes the account path.
- **Name**: the account uses the company name.
- **Company or sole trader**: can’t change once the euro account is set up.
- **Team and limits**: see [Organisations](../business/organisations-and-plans.md).
- **Passkey and recovery**: same as a personal account; see [Security and recovery](security-and-recovery.md).
