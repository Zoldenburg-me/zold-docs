# Assets, cost basis and reports

Business → **Assets** shows each asset per wallet with quantity, current reporting-currency value and cost basis.

- Each acquisition creates a tax lot (date, quantity, cost). Disposals consume lots first-in, first-out by default; Settings sets the method.
- Realised gain or loss appears on the disposing transaction.
- Euros deposited carry face-value cost, so the euro account alone creates no gain or loss. Conversions, USDC deposits and imported wallets can.
- **Reports → Monthly balance** gives month-end balances by wallet, network and token at month-end rates, as CSV.
- Home's **Recent activity** shows period profit and loss and top assets.

These are bookkeeping figures, not tax advice; your accountant decides how to report holdings and gains.
