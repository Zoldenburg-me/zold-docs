# Tester checklist

Tick these off on app.zoldhq.com. Setup details are in [Test Zold on mainnet](test-on-mainnet.md).

{% hint style="warning" %}
Real money moves. Use 1 € for every payment test.
{% endhint %}

## Before you start

- [ ] Wrote to aurel@zoldhq.com and got Cloudflare Access to app.zoldhq.com
- [ ] Have a verified Monerium account at monerium.app
- [ ] Created a **Private** app under **Developers** and saved its client ID and secret

## Set up

- [ ] Created a Zold account with your email
- [ ] Set up your passkey
- [ ] Went through the recovery step
- [ ] Chose **Add Monerium API keys** and pasted the client ID and secret
- [ ] Pressed **Get your IBAN** and approved with your passkey
- [ ] Your IBAN shows on **Home**

## Money in

- [ ] Sent 1 € from your bank to your Zold IBAN
- [ ] The 1 € shows as your balance in Zold
- [ ] The payment shows in your activity and statement

## Money out

- [ ] Sent 1 € by SEPA bank transfer to your own bank account
- [ ] Approved it with your passkey
- [ ] It arrived at your bank, and Zold shows it as paid

## Get paid

- [ ] Opened your payment page and shared the link
- [ ] Issued an invoice to yourself and opened its Pay button

## Finish

- [ ] Sent anything odd to Aurel: time, what you pressed, a screenshot
- [ ] Removed the API keys in Zold, if you stop testing
- [ ] Deleted the Private app under **Developers** at Monerium, if you stop testing
