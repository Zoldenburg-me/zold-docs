# How a payment works

Choose **Send** and follow the five screens.

## Steps

1. **Where:** choose an open destination. Europe is available for SEPA. Kenya cash pickup appears only when its payout partner is live.
2. **How:** choose the method offered for that destination.
3. **How much:** enter an amount. Zold quotes the recipient amount, fee, live mid-market rate and margin. The quote lasts **10 minutes**. If no fresh rate is available, Zold refuses to quote; it never uses an old rate.
4. **Who:** choose a contact or enter recipient details. Bank transfers need a name and IBAN, with an optional reference. Cash pickup needs the recipient's ID name and mobile number.
5. **Confirm:** check the amounts sent and received, rate, fee, recipient and reference, then approve with your passkey. Approval binds the exact amount, recipient identity and account; the same approval moves the money.

The transfer continues if you leave. Track its real state in **Activity**.

If a step fails after funds leave, Zold reverses what it can and returns the funds at the current rate, showing deductions. If a person must review it, the transfer says **Manual review** and you are contacted. See [Transfer states](../reference/transfer-states.md).
