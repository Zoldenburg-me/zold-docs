# Crypto deposit

Open **Add money → Crypto wallet** and copy your account address or scan its QR code.

Send only **USDC on Base**. Other tokens or networks are not recognised by the app. They remain in your account, but Zold cannot see or move them for you.

## Convert to euros

USDC counts as received when it arrives. Its euro value uses that day's ECB reference rate; an invoice or payment link it pays is marked paid at that value. **Settings → Main currency** controls what happens next:

| Main currency | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Euros, ask me to convert | USDC waits under **Waiting to convert**. Choose **Convert**, check the price and approve. |
| Euros, keep them as dollars | USDC stays under **Kept as dollars**; convert later if you choose. |
| Digital dollars (USDC) | USDC stays as dollars. |

Nothing converts without your approval. If the amount would fall below the quote's floor, nothing converts. Zold charges no conversion fee; you are credited the measured amount. Any unconverted remainder stays as USDC. While a conversion is **Checking**, it cannot be requested again.

EURe sent from another wallet is credited as received. On a business account, deposits are book entries dated on arrival; a later USDC conversion adds a second entry with its gain or loss.

For other tokens or networks, use your [payment page](../get-paid/payment-page.md), which lists what it accepts.
