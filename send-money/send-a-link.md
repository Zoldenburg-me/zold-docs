# Send a link

Share a link, and the person you owe fills in the amount and where to send it. You approve the payment before anything leaves your account.

## Steps

1. Go to **Send → Send a link**.
2. In **Up to (euros)**, enter the most you are willing to pay.
3. Add a **Note for them** if you like, then press **Create link**.
4. Copy or **Share** the link.
5. They open it, say how much, what it was for, and give an IBAN or a crypto wallet.
6. On **Home**, open the request under **Send links to pay**, then press **Pay**, or **Decline** to refuse it.
7. Check the payment and approve with your passkey.

## Good to know

- **The limit is yours**: they never see it. A request over it is refused, and they are asked for a smaller amount.
- **Copy it now**: Zold shows the link once and keeps no readable copy of it.
- **Amount**: pay exactly what they asked, to the details they gave. Anything else is refused before it is sent.
- **Too many tries**: after five amounts over your limit, the link stops taking answers for a day. They are asked to get a new link from you.
- **Where it goes**: an IBAN by SEPA bank transfer, or a wallet in EURe on Base. They choose.
- **Who can send one**: personal accounts with a personal space for invoices. The app offers to set it up.

{% hint style="warning" %}
A wallet payment can't be called back. Check the address with them before you approve.
{% endhint %}
