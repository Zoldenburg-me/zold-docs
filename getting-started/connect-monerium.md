# Get your IBAN

Your IBAN comes with one identity check and one passkey approval. Zold runs no separate identity check and does not pass on your signup details.

## Steps

1. After setting up your passkey, choose **Sign up or sign in with Monerium**, our IBAN partner.
2. Sign in or open an account there and complete its check: details, document and liveness. Usually minutes; it emails you if it needs more.
3. Approve the connection and return to Zold.
4. Press **Get your IBAN** and approve with your passkey.

You can leave during the check; the IBAN appears on Home when ready.

## Skipped it at signup?

1. Open **Profile → Monerium**. In Zold Business: **Accounts** or **Settings → Monerium**, then **Connect Monerium**.
2. Press **Continue with Monerium** and follow the steps above.
3. A company account returns to **Accounts**: press **Connect the company’s IBAN**.

## Already have a Monerium IBAN?

Each Monerium profile has one IBAN. To have it pay into Zold, choose **Use your Monerium IBAN in Zold**, type `MOVE` and approve. Payers keep the same IBAN; your old wallet stops receiving. You can move it back through Monerium.

## Use your own Monerium API keys

On the Monerium screen, under **Developer option: your own API keys**, enter your developer app's client ID and secret. Zold checks the pair and stores it encrypted; the secret is never shown again.

## Good to know

- **Before the IBAN**: your account works as a wallet: hold dollars and euros, convert dollars to euros, get paid through your payment page, send invoices. Bank transfers need the IBAN.
- **Declined**: your wallet stays open without an IBAN. Try another Monerium account or write to support@zoldhq.com.
- **Activation failed**: press **Get your IBAN** again.
- **Removing API keys**: your IBAN stays on record but deposits and payouts pause.
- **Changing the connection**: needs your passkey and signs you out on other devices.
