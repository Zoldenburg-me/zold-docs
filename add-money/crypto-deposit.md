# Crypto deposit

Open **Add money → Crypto wallet**. The screen shows your account address as text and a QR code of that same address.

* **Network:** Base.
* **Token:** USDC.

Send USDC on Base to that address from any wallet or exchange. It arrives in your account directly; no one is in between.

{% hint style="danger" %}
Send only **USDC on the Base network**. Tokens sent on another network, or other tokens sent to this address, are not recognised by the app. They are not lost, since the account is yours, but Zold cannot see them or move them for you.
{% endhint %}

## Converting to euros

A USDC payment counts as received the moment it arrives. It is valued in euros at the ECB reference rate of that day, and an invoice or payment link it pays is marked paid at that value. Whether you then keep the dollars or convert them is up to you.

Your **main currency** decides what happens next (**Settings → Main currency**):

* **Euros, ask me to convert:** the payment waits under **Waiting to convert** on the crypto wallet screen. Tap **Convert**, check the price, and approve with Face ID or your fingerprint.
* **Euros, keep them as dollars:** the payment stays as USDC under **Kept as dollars**. You can convert it any time from the same screen.
* **Digital dollars (USDC):** the payment stays as USDC and there is nothing to convert.

Nothing converts without your approval. The price you see comes with a floor: if less than that would arrive, nothing converts and your dollars stay where they are. What actually arrives is what you are credited, and Zold charges no fee for converting. If only part of a payment converts, the rest stays as USDC and you can convert it later.

If a conversion was sent but its result is not confirmed yet, the payment shows **Checking** and is not offered for conversion again until it is clear.

Euros (EURe) sent to your address from another wallet are credited as they are. On a business account, every crypto deposit is also a line in the organisation's books, dated by when it arrived on the network. Converting held USDC later adds a second line with the gain or loss against the value on arrival.

## Paying you by link

If you want someone else to send you crypto without handing them a raw address, or to take USDC from networks other than Base, use your [payment page](../get-paid/payment-page.md). It has its own address and lists every token and network it takes.
