# Eligibility

Zold is unavailable to US persons, residents of sanctioned countries, and residents of some countries. The app tells you at account creation, before anything is stored.

## Not available

| Who | |
| --- | --- |
| US citizens, Green Card holders and US tax residents, wherever they live | Not available |
| Residents of a US territory | Not available |
| Residents or citizens of Iran, North Korea, Syria, Cuba, Russia or Belarus | Not available |
| Residents of Myanmar, Afghanistan, Libya, Yemen, Venezuela, Iraq or Lebanon | Not available |
| Companies incorporated in the US, or with a US owner of 25% or more | Not available |

## What Zold checks

- **People**: residence, every citizenship and US-person status.
- **Companies**: also country of incorporation and whether an owner of 25% or more is a US person.

Everyone else can open an account; what kind is on [Supported countries](supported-countries.md).
