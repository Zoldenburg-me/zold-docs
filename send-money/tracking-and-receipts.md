# Tracking and receipts

## Activity and details

**Activity** lists deposits and transfers, newest first, with a status on each row. 

- **Bank deposit**: sender, reference and date.
- **Crypto deposit**: sending wallet, network date and current asset. Converted dollars show euros received; held dollars offer **Convert to euros**.
- **Transfer**: amount sent and received, rate, fee, recipient, reference or pickup code, and a timeline of states reached. Cash pickups also show the MoneyGram reference and **Refresh status**.

A conversion is your own money changing form, not another received payment. Technical details link to Basescan.

## Share a receipt

Open a transfer after it starts moving and choose **Share receipt**. Pick what the link reveals:

| Field | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| Your or recipient name | Full, first, last or none |
| Payout account | Full, short or hidden |
| Amounts | Both, sent or received |

Optional switches add **Rate, fee & margin**, **Your reference** and **Settlement route**. In Business, open **Statements → Receipts for payments → Share**.

- **Access**: anyone with the link can open it without an account. Withheld fields are not sent.
- **One link per transfer**: sharing again replaces its settings.
- **Expiry**: links expire after **30 days** and can be revoked; visitors see that it was revoked.
- **Care**: share links only with the intended person.
