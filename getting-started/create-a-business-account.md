# Create a business account

For a company. For an account in your own name, see [Create a personal account](create-your-account.md).

## Steps

1. Open **zoldhq.com/app**, choose **Create an account**, then **Business**.
2. The Zold narwhal asks one question at a time; type in the chat bar or tap a reply. First the company:
   - **Legal name**, and a trading name if customers know it by another.
   - **Country of incorporation**.
3. Then you: **Name** (as on your ID), **Residence country**, **Email**.
4. Then:
   - **Company US question**: incorporated in the US, or a US owner of 25% or more?
   - **Your US question**: are you a US citizen, Green Card holder or US tax resident?
   - Confirm you may open the account for the company, and accept Zold's Terms.
5. Add a way back in, or **Skip for now**.
6. Set up your passkey, then **approve once more**.
7. On Home, finish **Confirm your email** and [Get your IBAN](connect-monerium.md).

## Good to know

- **Change an answer**: tap it under the narwhal, and it asks that question again.

- **Who can join**: see [Eligibility](../reference/eligibility.md). A US incorporation or a US owner of 25% or more closes the account path.
- **Name**: the account uses the company name.
- **Team and limits**: see [Organisations](../business/organisations-and-plans.md).
- **Passkey and recovery**: same as a personal account; see [Security and recovery](security-and-recovery.md).
