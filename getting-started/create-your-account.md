# Create a personal account

For an account as an individual. For a business, including a sole trader, see [Create a business account](create-a-business-account.md).

## Steps

1. Open **zoldhq.com/app**, choose **Create an account**, then **Personal**.
2. Answer two questions: your **name**, as on your ID, and your **email**.
3. Add a way back in: tap **Google**, **Apple** or **Email**, or **Skip for now**.
4. Press **Set up Face ID**, or **Set up biometrics** on a computer. Approve the prompt (Face ID, fingerprint, Touch ID, Windows Hello, device PIN, phone or security key), then **approve once more**. Setting up sign-in accepts Zold's Terms.
5. On Home, press **Answer** next to **Where you live**: pick your country and answer the US question (US citizen, Green Card holder or US tax resident?), then **Save**.
6. On Home, finish **Confirm your email** and [Get your IBAN](connect-monerium.md).

{% hint style="info" %}
A "yes" to the US question closes the account path wherever you live, and the answer is recorded.
{% endhint %}

## Good to know

- **Who can join**: Zold is unavailable to US persons, residents of sanctioned countries, and residents of some countries. See [Eligibility](../reference/eligibility.md).
- **Passkey**: the only approver; Zold cannot replace it. Without sync (iCloud Keychain, Google Password Manager or another), losing the device means recovery.
- **Recovery helper**: can move your account to a new phone, never send money.
- **Cost**: account creation is free and fixes your address.
- **Before "Where you live"**: the account has its sign-in and nothing else. Sending, adding money and getting paid open once you answer.
- **Invoices before "Where you live"**: **Invoice** and **Set up your personal space** ask it first, then bring you back to the set-up.
- **Before your IBAN**: Zold works as a wallet: hold dollars and euros, get paid through your payment page, send invoices. Bank transfers need the IBAN. Wallet accounts have no IBAN step.
- **Name**: editable in **Profile** until your identity is verified; then locked as the IBAN holder's name.
