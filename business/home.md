# Business Home

Home is the first screen of Zold Business. It shows your balance, what needs you, and recent money in and out.

## How it works

- **Quick send**: pick a contact with a saved IBAN and enter an amount. Press **Review transfer** to open **New payment** filled in. Nothing is sent until you submit it there. If Quick send is greyed out, the line under it says why.
- **Security**: shows whether your passkey signs, recovery is on, and a backup key is added. **Add** and **Set up** open the app.
- **Set up**: the five steps to a working account, with "N of 5 done". It disappears once all five are done. Only the company's own login sees **Security** and **Set up**.
- **No IBAN yet**: the **Accounts** card says why. At a sole trader, press **Connect Monerium**, then **Activate IBAN**; Monerium verifies the business before it issues an IBAN. For a company, the account stays not open until issuing its IBAN onto its Safe is live; the card on Home says so.
- **Recent activity**: if your role allows, **Pending** lists payments saved, in review, approved or being sent, and **Export CSV** downloads all transactions.

## Good to know

- **Couldn’t check**: if Zold cannot read who signs or the recovery state, the card says so. It never shows that as on or off.
- **Balance**: only the account backed by your own login shows a figure. Zold shows no combined company balance. A business set up as a company shows no figure: while its Safe or IBAN is not live, the card says which; once the IBAN is live it shows the IBAN. If Zold cannot read the company's account, the card says that, not that no account is connected; reload to try again.
