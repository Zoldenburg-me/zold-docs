# FAQ

**Is Zold a bank?**
No. Zold is an app built by Zoldenburg. Your IBAN and your euros come from Monerium EMI ehf., an electronic money institution licensed in Iceland and supervised by the Central Bank of Iceland. Under the EU E-Money Directive (2009/110/EC), that licence is passported across the European Economic Area, so Monerium can serve you anywhere in the EEA. Zold holds no licence of its own: Monerium looks after the regulated money, and Zold builds the software you use it with. Only you can approve what leaves your account.

**Can anyone send money from my account except me?**
No. Every transfer is approved with your passkey on your own device (Face ID or your fingerprint on a phone), for that amount and that recipient. Someone who has your email or password, someone posing as a bank employee on the phone, and Zold itself cannot approve one. If anyone asks you to approve a transfer you did not start, don't.

**Could a thief empty my account with my phone?**
Not with the phone alone. Every transfer needs your face or fingerprint, or your phone's passcode where the phone falls back to it, so keep a passcode only you know. If your phone is lost or stolen, [recovery](../getting-started/security-and-recovery.md) moves your account to a new phone after a short wait.

**What if Zold's servers were hacked?**
Our servers hold no key to your account, so a break-in alone can't send your money. The app is loaded from our servers, though, so someone who took them over could try to show you one payment and ask you to approve another. Approve only payments you started yourself, and check the amount and recipient each time. What a break-in could expose is the data we keep, such as your email and your payment history.

**How does Zold make money?**
From paid plans: Premium for people and Business for companies, each with a 30-day free trial. Starter is free. Sending euros, receiving them and converting to and from digital dollars carry no Zold fee, and Zold earns nothing by lending your money out, because it doesn't lend it. See [Fees and exchange rates](../fees/fees-and-rates.md) and [Organisations and plans](../business/organisations-and-plans.md).

**Do I have to leave my bank?**
No. Many people start by having their salary or client payments land in Zold. Send money to your other bank any time.

**My employer only takes German IBANs.**
In the euro area, an employer can't refuse an IBAN because of the country it's from. Your Zold IBAN works for salary like any other.

**Can people abroad pay me?**
Yes. By euro bank transfer from anywhere in Europe, or in USDC (digital dollars) from outside Europe, through your [payment link](../get-paid/payment-links.md).

**Do I earn interest?**
No. Zold does not lend your euros out, so there is no rate to promise.

**Is my money covered by deposit insurance?**
No. E-money is not covered by a deposit guarantee scheme. Instead, the issuer must safeguard the full value of every euro it has issued, and you have a legal right to redeem at par. Read Monerium's terms for the detail.

**Can Zold freeze or move my money?**
No. Every transfer is approved on your device, and Zold holds no key to your account. Recovery is your choice: if you pick Zoldenburg for recovery, it can move your account to your new phone after verifying it's you, but never send money, and you can cancel the move from your old phone during the waiting period. Add a backup key of your own, such as a hardware wallet, and Zold has no part in it at all. The one other party is Monerium: as the issuer of your euros, it can freeze them where the law requires it.

**What happens if Zold shuts down?**
If you added a backup key of your own, your account is yours even without Zold: you can move your money with Safe's open tools at [app.safe.global](https://app.safe.global). Without a backup key, your passkey works only through zoldhq.com, so you depend on Zold's sign-in. Either way, your right to redeem your euros is against Monerium, not Zold. See [How Zold works under the hood](how-it-works.md).

**Do I need to buy crypto?**
No. You use euros. There are no network fees for you to pay.

**Do card payments need Face ID too?**
No. The card spends from its own card account, like any card. The approval rule covers transfers from your Zold account. See [Gnosis Pay card](../add-ons/gnosis-pay-card.md).

**Can I have more than one device?**
Yes, if your passkey syncs (iCloud Keychain, Google Password Manager, a password manager). Each device also has its own payment key, created the first time you use the account there.

**I lost my phone.**
See [Security and recovery](../getting-started/security-and-recovery.md). If you chose Zoldenburg for recovery, start a recovery from the sign-in screen and contact support with the reference it shows; we check your identity and sign it. If you did not choose recovery, Zoldenburg UG cannot recover your account — only your euro balance can be recovered, from Monerium.

**Why was my quote refused?**
Usually because a fresh market rate could not be fetched, or because the venue price was too far from the market. Try again in a minute. Zold never quotes from an old rate.

**Why does my balance say "Balance unavailable"?**
Zold reads your balance from the network each time it loads your account. When that read fails, the app says so instead of showing €0.00, and won't start a transfer until it can check the amount against your balance. Your money hasn't moved. Reload in a minute.

**Why did my transfer go to Manual review?**
Something happened that should not be resolved automatically, such as a partner failing after it already held the funds. Your money is accounted for and someone will look at it. You will be contacted.

**Can I hold dollars?**
Soon. A US dollar account with its own account and routing number is being added through Bridge, and sits in the same account as your euros. See [Your US dollar account](../add-money/usd-account.md).

**Can I pay in a currency other than euros?**
Bank transfers out are in euros. Cash pickup in Kenya, paid out in shillings, opens when its payout partner is live. Other currencies open as their accounts become available; see [Accounts and currencies](../business/accounts-and-currencies.md).

**Is my payment page private?**
No. See [Your payment page](../get-paid/payment-page.md).

**Does Zold give tax advice?**
No. The invoicing tools stop you from producing an invalid document under the rules Zold encodes, and say exactly what they do not check. Your accountant decides the rest.
