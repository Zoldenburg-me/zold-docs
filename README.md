# Welcome to Zold

**Nothing moves without your yes.**

Zold is an app for holding, sending and receiving euros. What it sells is approval: a transfer leaves your account only when you approve it, for that amount, to that recipient.

## Three things that hold

**Only you can approve a transfer.** Not a scammer who got hold of your password, not someone on the phone posing as your bank, and not Zold. You approve each transfer with Face ID or your fingerprint, and the approval covers the exact amount and recipient on the screen. Zold holds no key to your account. Card payments work differently: see [Gnosis Pay card](add-ons/gnosis-pay-card.md).

**In a company, no one person approves alone.** With review switched on, which comes with the Business plan, the payer prepares a payment, a second person approves it, the bookkeeper sorts the books but cannot send, and the accountant gets the books, not the keys. See [Members and roles](business/members-and-roles.md) and [Payments and approvals](business/payments-and-approvals.md).

**No rate. No asterisk.** Zold does not lend your euros out, so there is no interest rate to promise. Your euros are e-money, backed one-for-one and held in your name. E-money is not covered by a deposit guarantee scheme.

## What Zold is

Zold is an app, built by Zoldenburg. It is not a bank. Your IBAN and your euros come from Monerium, a licensed e-money institution. Zold is the software you use them with. [How Zold works under the hood](reference/how-it-works.md) names every part.
