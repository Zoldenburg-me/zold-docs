# Your payment page

Your public link, **zoldhq.com/pay/yourname**, shows a QR code and address. The payer scans it with their own wallet; there is no amount field or wallet connection.

## Claim a handle

Profile → **Payment page**. You can claim one as soon as your account exists.

- 3–30 characters: lowercase Latin letters, digits and internal hyphens; unique.
- Cannot start with `0x`, match an app route, or use Zold, partner, staff-role or well-known-person names, even inside a longer handle.
- Set a display name, or the page shows your handle: up to 40 characters, no emoji, and nothing that could pass for someone else's name.

## What the payer sees

Your address, QR code, **open in wallet** link and accepted tokens, networks, minimums and bridge fees (also under **Get paid → Your page**). USDC on Base arrives directly; USDC from other listed networks arrives as USDC on Base.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Send only a listed token on its listed network, and at least its minimum. Other transfers do not reach your account automatically.
{% endhint %}

## Privacy

The page is public. Every payment lands at one address; anyone with the link can look up its history on a public explorer, and anyone who knows or guesses your handle can find the page.

For a private request, send a [receipt](../send-money/tracking-and-receipts.md) or [Invoice-Me link](invoice-me-links.md). For a specific amount and description, create a [payment link](payment-links.md).
