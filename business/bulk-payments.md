# Bulk payments

One payment can pay several bank payees, each with their own amount and note. Every organisation.

## Steps

1. Go to **New payment**.
2. Under **To**, pick every contact you're paying.
3. Under **Amounts**, enter each payee's **Amount in euros** and, if you like, a **Note**.
4. Press **Save payment** (or **Submit for approval** where review is on).

Each payee becomes one line of the payment. It follows the same [review and sending](payments-and-approvals.md) as a one-payee payment.

## Good to know

- **Saved contacts only**: every payee is a contact with a saved IBAN, so a change to their bank details is caught at review and sending.
- **Limit**: up to 200 payees per payment. From a company's IBAN, up to 10: the owner signs each one with their passkey. See [company bank payouts](company-bank-payouts.md#in-a-payment-run).
- **Same IBAN, same amount**: from a company's IBAN, two payees can't get the same amount at the same IBAN. Pay it once, with the total, or in a separate payment.
- **Each payee once**: to pay a contact twice, enter the total.
- **Crypto wallet**: a payment to a wallet has one payee.
- **No CSV import**: the app has no file upload for payees.
